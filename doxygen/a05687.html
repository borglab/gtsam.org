gtsam::gnss::DoubleDifferenceData Struct Reference

Detailed Description

Shared geometry data for double-difference factors.

Holds the four undifferenced observations (rover/base, ref/target satellite), the corresponding satellite ECEF positions at rover and base observation times, and the known base station ECEF position. The struct owns the computation of the observed double-difference and of the geometric DD model range (with Sagnac-corrected geodist) plus its Jacobian w.r.t. the rover or antenna ECEF position.

Used by both the pseudorange and carrier-phase DD factors so the DD geometry/Jacobian implementation is shared.

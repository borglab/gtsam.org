28// Opinionated composition of LocationRecovery for the bipartite
29// camera+landmark estimation problem (GLOMAP-style global positioning).
30// Enforces:
31// - Explicit separation of camera keys and landmark keys
32// - Validation that the anchor key is a camera
33// - BILINEAR (BATA) cost function exclusively
34// - No DSFMap handling (not applicable to camera-landmark edges)
35//
36// For custom graph configurations (mixed edges, custom gauge, etc.),
37// use LocationRecovery directly.
38//
39// Reference: Pan, L. et al., "Global Structure-from-Motion Revisited,"
40// ECCV 2024.

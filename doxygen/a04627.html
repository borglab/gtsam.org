◆ basis()

const Matrix32 & gtsam::Unit3::basis ( OptionalJacobian< 6, 2 > H = {}) const

Returns the local coordinate frame to tangent plane It is a 3*2 matrix [b1 b2] composed of two orthogonal directions tangent to the sphere at the current direction.

Provides derivatives of the basis with the two basis vectors stacked up as a 6x1.

Local coordinates with derivatives in the tangent bases of this and s.

Derivatives are undefined at the antipode; requesting them there throws std::domain_error. The value-only overload retains its antipodal convention.

gtsam::traits< Unit3 > Struct Reference

Detailed Description

Define GTSAM traits.

QCQP traits for the unit sphere S^2.

The lift is a relaxation of the sphere: at D = 3 the row is a unit vector in R^3, and at D > 3 it is in S^{D-1}, which is the lifted sphere the Burer-Monteiro method searches over.

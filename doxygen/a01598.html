Detailed Description

Quadratic range factor.

Ternary factor over the two positions t_i and target, plus one auxiliary direction u per measurement. The raw residual (||target - t_i|| - range)^2 is not polynomial and cannot enter a QCQP; the auxiliary direction makes the term quadratic:

min nu ||target - t_i - range * u||^2 over t_i, target in R^d, u in R^d subject to ||u||^2 = 1

Its minimum over the unit sphere is the raw residual, so the reformulation is exact (see [1]).

The auxiliary uses Rot2 in 2D and Unit3 in 3D; in 2D the unit vector is the rotation's first column, u = R * e_1.
